## How this list is made

Every role comes from a company's public job board (Greenhouse, Lever, Ashby, SmartRecruiters, Workable, Keka,
Zoho Recruit, Workday and others) and from Y Combinator's India listings. A script reads them every morning,
keeps the roles in Indian cities or remote-India, and sorts them by experience asked. Closed roles drop off the
next day. Experience levels are read from the title and description, so a few will be off: check the posting.
